Check-in is online with Vikey: before arrival you receive a link to upload the documents of all guests (required by Italian law). If you can't find it, check your spam folder.

We bring the keys to you in person at the apartment, Via La Spezia 71: you don't have to pick them up anywhere. Please message us when you are on your way so we can be there with the keys.

Getting here

Metro: Lodi (Line C) is right on Via La Spezia, about 1 minute on foot. San Giovanni (Lines A and C) and Re di Roma (Line A) are also a few minutes' walk.

From Termini: Metro A towards Anagnina to San Giovanni (3 stops), then Metro C towards Monte Compatri/Pantano for 1 stop to Lodi. About 20 minutes, or about 30 on foot.

From Fiumicino: Leonardo Express train to Termini (about 32 minutes, every 15; ticket at the station or online), then the metro as above. The metro ticket is separate.

From Ciampino: shuttle bus to Termini, then the metro as above.

Taxi: only use official white taxis from the taxi rank. We are just outside the Aurelian Walls, so the fixed airport–centre fare does not apply: the meter is used.

Parking: street parking is paid (blue lines) but hard to find. Alternative: Garage Concordia, Via Concordia 44 (near Via Appia Nuova, open 24 hours, covered and supervised; you leave the keys with the staff). You can book online, e.g. on ParkingMyCar or Parclick.

The Basilica of St John Lateran is within walking distance.
